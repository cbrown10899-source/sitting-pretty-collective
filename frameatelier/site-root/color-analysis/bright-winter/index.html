Bright Winter

also called Clear Winter

The highest-chroma season in the system. Bright Winter sits beside Bright Spring — both need extreme saturation — but Bright Winter's brights are cooled rather than warmed.

Wear

  • Electric, saturated cool color
  • Black paired with a single vivid bright
  • Icy pastels as accents, not as the whole outfit

Avoid

  • Muted earth tones — camel, rust, moss
  • Dusty pastels
  • Low-contrast tonal dressing
