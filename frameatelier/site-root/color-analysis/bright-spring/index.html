Bright Spring

also called Clear Spring

Warmth carried at full saturation. Bright Spring sits between Bright Winter and True Spring — it keeps Winter's clarity but tips warm, so colors read vivid and lit rather than icy.

Metals

Yellow gold first. Bright polished silver works where a softer season's would not — the shine is the point.

Wear

  • Saturated warm brights worn at full strength
  • Clear contrast between light and bright, not light and dark
  • Coral and turquoise together — the season's signature pairing

Avoid

  • Dusty, grayed or chalky tones — they flatten you immediately
  • Head-to-toe black
  • Muted earth tones like moss and rust

Not sure? Check the neighbors

Most mistyping happens between adjacent seasons. These are the two most likely to be confused with Bright Spring.

Bright SpringBright Winter

Bright Spring or Bright Winter? Hold clear yellow and icy pink side by side under daylight. If yellow lifts the face and icy pink drains it, you're Bright Spring. Winter takes the icy pink.

Bright SpringTrue Spring

Bright Spring or True Spring? Both are warm. The question is saturation: if softened, slightly dusty warm tones still look good on you, you're True Spring. If anything less than full brightness looks tired, you're Bright.

Makeup

Coral and warm pink lips, peach or apricot blush, clear warm bronze on the eye. Avoid brown-based nudes.
