Light Spring

The lightest warm season. Light Spring sits next to Light Summer, and the two share delicacy — but Light Spring's palette is warmed with gold where Light Summer's is cooled with blue.

Metals

Light yellow gold and rose gold. Keep the metal delicate — heavy antique gold is too much weight.

Wear

  • Light, warm, gently saturated color
  • Tonal light-on-light outfits rather than high contrast
  • Ivory instead of stark white

Avoid

  • Black and charcoal
  • Deep burgundy, forest, espresso — anything heavy
  • Strong dark-light contrast

Not sure? Check the neighbors

Most mistyping happens between adjacent seasons. These are the two most likely to be confused with Light Spring.

Light SpringLight Summer

Light Spring or Light Summer? Compare peach against soft rose at the jaw. If peach warms the skin and rose grays it, you're Light Spring.

Makeup

Peach and soft coral lips, warm light pink blush, champagne and soft gold eyes. Nothing smoky.
