Soft Summer

The most muted cool season, and one of the most commonly mistyped. Soft Summer's defining feature is not coolness but softness — every color has gray mixed through it.

Metals

Brushed or matte silver, pewter, oxidised finishes. Avoid anything highly polished.

Wear

  • Grayed, dusty color at medium depth
  • Blended, low-contrast outfits
  • Soft white and oyster instead of pure white

Avoid

  • Saturated brights — they wear you rather than the reverse
  • Black and pure white together
  • Sharp high-contrast pairings

Not sure? Check the neighbors

Most mistyping happens between adjacent seasons. These are the two most likely to be confused with Soft Summer.

Soft SummerTrue Summer

Soft Summer or True Summer? Saturation, not temperature. If clear cool rose looks good, you're True Summer. If it looks garish next to dusty rose, you're Soft.

Soft Summer or Soft Autumn? Both are muted — the split is temperature. Sage against dusty rose: if sage yellows the skin and rose evens it, you're Soft Summer.

Makeup

Dusty rose and soft mauve lips, muted pink blush, taupe and soft plum eyes. Nothing high-shine.
