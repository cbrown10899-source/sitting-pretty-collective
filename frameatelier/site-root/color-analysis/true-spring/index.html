True Spring

also called Warm Spring

The warmest season in the system. True Spring is golden all the way through — no cool undertone anywhere in the palette, and no need for the extreme brightness Bright Spring demands.

Metals

Yellow gold, brass, warm copper. Silver reads cold and cheap against this coloring.

Wear

  • Golden, sunlit color at medium-high saturation
  • Warm neutrals as the base instead of gray or black
  • Ivory and cream in place of white

Avoid

  • Black — it hollows the face out
  • Icy pastels and anything with a blue undertone
  • Gray as a wardrobe neutral

Not sure? Check the neighbors

Most mistyping happens between adjacent seasons. These are the two most likely to be confused with True Spring.

True SpringLight Spring

True Spring or Light Spring? Value is the test. If deep golden brown and poppy red hold their own on you, you're True Spring. If they overwhelm and you look better in peach and buttercup, you're Light.

Makeup

Warm coral and peach lips, apricot blush, golden and warm-copper eyes. Skip cool berry shades.
