True Winter

also called Cool Winter

The coldest, clearest season. True Winter is built on pure hues and stark contrast — the only season where black and optic white together are genuinely correct.

Wear

  • Pure, clear, cool color
  • Maximum contrast — black with optic white
  • Jewel tones at full saturation

Avoid

  • Beige, camel, olive, rust
  • Anything dusty or grayed
  • Warm gold tones near the face
